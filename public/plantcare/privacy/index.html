PlantCare Journal Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

PlantCare Journal ("the app") is a plant care app developed by Edwin Ramos Madera, an independent developer based in the Dominican Republic ("I", "the developer"). The app is free to download and offers an optional paid Premium subscription. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and what control you have over it.

1. Summary

  • PlantCare Journal does not require an account or sign-in.
  • The developer does not operate any servers and does not receive or store your plants, photos, notes or other content.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no Firebase.
  • Your plants, journal and photos are stored only on your device, unless you choose to export a backup.
  • Only when you use an AI feature (diagnosis, assistant or evolution report) is the necessary information sent directly to the Google Gemini API, using an API key that you provide yourself.
  • If you subscribe to Premium, the purchase is handled by Google Play, and the subscription status is checked through RevenueCat, a service provider (see section 5).

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores locally, in its private storage, the information you enter:

  • Registered plants (name, species, soil, lighting, location, health status, watering frequency) and their photos.
  • Care tasks (watering, pruning, fertilizing, etc.), reminder settings and their history.
  • Plant journal entries, notes, tags, humidity estimates, and photos.
  • Saved AI diagnoses and evolution reports.
  • App preferences (such as language), backup settings and a cached copy of your Premium status.
  • Your Google Gemini API key, if you set one. It is kept in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore-backed, through expo-secure-store), separate from the rest of the app data, and it is never included in backups.

This information never leaves your device except as described in sections 4, 5 and 6. The developer has no access to it.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos of your plants when you choose to (when adding a plant or a journal entry, or for a diagnosis). Photos are stored locally.
  • Photo selection: you can pick images from your gallery through the system photo picker; the app only accesses the images you select.
  • Notifications: to remind you about care tasks such as watering. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; the app uses no push server and requests no push token. You can turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Internet: to send AI requests to Google Gemini and, if you use Premium, to communicate with Google Play and RevenueCat.
  • Google Play billing: added by the Google Play Billing library so you can buy and restore the Premium subscription.
  • Clipboard: only when you tap paste or copy: the app reads the clipboard to paste your Gemini API key, and can copy an AI answer to it. Clipboard content is not stored or sent anywhere else.
  • Folder access (backups): only if you turn on automatic backups, you choose a folder through the Android system folder picker, and the app writes backup files only to that folder.

The app does not request access to your location, microphone, contacts, or other personal data.

4. AI features (Google Gemini)

The AI features are optional and require you to enter your own Google Gemini API key, obtained from Google AI Studio. Requests go directly from your device to Google (generativelanguage.googleapis.com, over encrypted HTTPS):

  • Diagnosis and assistant: when you request a diagnosis or ask a question, the app sends the plant photo you choose (if any), plant data (name, species, location, watering frequency, relevant history) and the text you write (symptoms, observations or questions).
  • Evolution report (Premium): when you create an evolution report for a plant, the app sends up to 6 photos of that plant, together with its saved diagnoses, journal entries and completed care tasks as text.
  • In every case, your API key is sent to authenticate the request.

This data does not pass through any server operated by the developer. Google's handling of it is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy, under the agreement between you and Google associated with your key. Note that under those terms, for unpaid tiers Google may use submitted content to improve its products. Do not send photos or text containing personal information you do not want to share with Google.

The evolution report is saved on your device. If you export it as a PDF, the PDF is created on your device and you choose where to share it through the Android share sheet; the developer does not receive it.

You can delete your saved key at any time from the Settings screen.

5. Premium subscription (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google: the app and the developer never see your card or payment details. Google's handling of your purchase is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) as a service provider. When billing is available, the app connects to RevenueCat, which receives:

  • An anonymous customer ID that RevenueCat generates for the app installation. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Your purchase history and subscription status for this app as reported by Google Play (product, purchase token, order ID, purchase and expiry dates).
  • Technical information that RevenueCat's SDK sends with each request, such as app version, device platform and OS version, and the IP address of the connection.

This information is used only to verify and restore your Premium subscription. RevenueCat processes it on the developer's behalf under its Privacy Policy. The developer can see this subscription data in the RevenueCat dashboard but does not use it for advertising or tracking.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or from the Premium screen in the app. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

6. Backups

  • Manual export and import: you can export all your app data (plants, tasks, journal, diagnoses, evolution reports, settings and photos) to a backup file and share or save it where you choose through the Android share sheet. You can restore it later by picking the file with the system file picker.
  • Weekly automatic backup (Premium): if you turn it on, the app writes a backup file about once a week to a folder you choose with the Android folder picker (Storage Access Framework), and keeps only the most recent copies you set.

Backup files do not contain your Gemini API key. They go only where you send them; the developer never receives them. If you save them to a cloud folder, that provider's terms apply. Keep them safe, as they contain your photos and notes.

7. Data sharing

The developer does not sell or rent your data and does not share it for advertising. Data reaches third parties only as described above: requests you start to Google Gemini (section 4), purchase handling by Google Play and subscription verification by RevenueCat as a service provider (section 5), and backup files you choose to share (section 6).

8. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Reset the app to a clean install from Settings. This deletes your plants, journal, photos, diagnoses, reports and API key, and cancels scheduled reminders.
  • Delete your API key from Settings.
  • Clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app, which deletes all of its local data.
  • Delete any backup files you exported, from the folder or service where you saved them.

Subscription data (RevenueCat): RevenueCat keeps subscription records while they are needed to provide and verify the subscription and as required by law. To request deletion, email edwinramos.93@gmail.com with the Google Play order ID of your purchase (it starts with GPA. and appears in your Google Play purchase receipt and order history), because the app does not show a customer ID. I will delete the matching customer record in RevenueCat within 30 days of your request at the latest, and confirm by email. Google keeps its own purchase records under the Google Privacy Policy.

For data already sent to Google Gemini, see Google's policies for your API key.

9. Security

Communication with Google Gemini, Google Play and RevenueCat uses HTTPS. Your API key is kept in the device's secure storage. Local data is protected by Android's app storage sandbox, and the app disables Android cloud backup of its data. We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock.

10. Children

PlantCare Journal is intended for adults 18 and over, in line with the Google Gemini API terms. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

12. Contact

Email: edwinramos.93@gmail.com

2. Información que se guarda en tu dispositivo

La app guarda localmente, en su almacenamiento interno, la información que tú introduces:

  • Plantas registradas (nombre, especie, sustrato, iluminación, ubicación, estado de salud, frecuencia de riego) y sus fotografías.
  • Tareas de cuidado (riego, poda, abono, etc.), la configuración de recordatorios y su historial.
  • Entradas del diario botánico, notas, etiquetas, estimaciones de humedad y fotografías.
  • Diagnósticos de IA e informes de evolución guardados.
  • Preferencias de la app (por ejemplo, el idioma), la configuración de copias de seguridad y una copia en caché de tu estado Premium.
  • Tu clave de API de Google Gemini, si la configuras. Se guarda en el almacenamiento seguro del dispositivo (respaldado por Android Keystore, mediante expo-secure-store), separada del resto de los datos, y nunca se incluye en las copias de seguridad.

Esta información no sale de tu dispositivo salvo en los casos descritos en las secciones 4, 5 y 6. El desarrollador no tiene acceso a ella.

  • Facturación de Google Play: lo añade la biblioteca de Google Play Billing para que puedas comprar y restaurar la suscripción Premium.
  • Portapapeles: solo cuando tocas pegar o copiar: la app lee el portapapeles para pegar tu clave de API de Gemini y puede copiar en él una respuesta de la IA. Ese contenido no se guarda ni se envía a ningún otro lugar.
  • Acceso a una carpeta (copias de seguridad): solo si activas las copias automáticas, eliges una carpeta con el selector de carpetas del sistema Android, y la app escribe las copias únicamente en esa carpeta.

La app no solicita acceso a tu ubicación, micrófono, contactos ni otros datos personales.

Las funciones de IA son opcionales y requieren que introduzcas tu propia clave de API de Google Gemini, obtenida en Google AI Studio. Las solicitudes van directamente desde tu dispositivo a Google (generativelanguage.googleapis.com, mediante conexión cifrada HTTPS):

  • Diagnóstico y asistente: cuando pides un diagnóstico o haces una consulta, la app envía la fotografía de la planta que elijas (si la hay), datos de la planta (nombre, especie, ubicación, frecuencia de riego, historial relevante) y el texto que escribas (síntomas, observaciones o preguntas).
  • Informe de evolución (Premium): cuando creas un informe de evolución de una planta, la app envía hasta 6 fotos de esa planta, junto con sus diagnósticos guardados, entradas del diario y tareas de cuidado completadas en forma de texto.

Estos datos no pasan por ningún servidor del desarrollador. Su tratamiento por parte de Google se rige por los Términos adicionales de la API de Gemini y la Política de Privacidad de Google, según el acuerdo entre tú y Google asociado a tu clave. Ten en cuenta que, según esos términos, en los niveles gratuitos Google puede usar el contenido enviado para mejorar sus productos. No envíes fotos ni textos con información personal que no quieras compartir con Google.

El informe de evolución se guarda en tu dispositivo. Si lo exportas como PDF, el PDF se crea en tu dispositivo y tú eliges dónde compartirlo con el menú de compartir de Android; el desarrollador no lo recibe.

Puedes eliminar tu clave guardada en cualquier momento desde la pantalla de Configuración.

Premium es una suscripción opcional de renovación automática que se vende mediante Google Play Billing. El pago lo procesa íntegramente Google: ni la app ni el desarrollador ven tu tarjeta ni tus datos de pago. Politique de confidentialité de PlantCare Journal

  • PlantCare Journal ne nécessite aucun compte ni connexion.

  • Les tâches d'entretien (arrosage, taille, engrais, etc.), les réglages des rappels et leur historique.

Ces données ne transitent par aucun serveur du développeur. Leur traitement par Google est régi par les Conditions d'utilisation supplémentaires de l'API Gemini et les Règles de confidentialité de Google, selon l'accord entre vous et Google associé à votre clé. 8. Conservation et suppression

Vos données restent sur votre appareil jusqu'à ce que vous les supprimiez. Vous pouvez :

  • Réinitialiser l'application (installation vierge) depuis les Paramètres. Cela supprime vos plantes, votre journal, vos photos, diagnostics, rapports et votre clé d'API, et annule les rappels programmés.
  • Supprimer votre clé d'API depuis les Paramètres.
  • Effacer les données de l'application dans les paramètres Android, ou désinstaller l'application, ce qui supprime toutes ses données locales.
  • Supprimer les sauvegardes que vous avez exportées, depuis le dossier ou le service où vous les avez enregistrées.

Données d'abonnement (RevenueCat) : RevenueCat conserve les enregistrements d'abonnement tant qu'ils sont nécessaires pour fournir et vérifier l'abonnement et dans la mesure exigée par la loi. Pour en demander la suppression, écrivez à edwinramos.93@gmail.com en indiquant le numéro de commande Google Play de votre achat (il commence par GPA. et figure sur votre reçu d'achat et dans l'historique des commandes Google Play), car l'application n'affiche pas d'identifiant client. Je supprimerai l'enregistrement client correspondant dans RevenueCat dans un délai maximal de 30 jours à compter de votre demande, et vous le confirmerai par e-mail. Google conserve ses propres enregistrements d'achat selon les Règles de confidentialité de Google.

Pour les données déjà envoyées à Google Gemini, consultez les règles de Google applicables à votre clé d'API.

9. Sécurité

Les communications avec Google Gemini, Google Play et RevenueCat utilisent HTTPS. Votre clé d'API est conservée dans le stockage sécurisé de l'appareil. Les données locales sont protégées par le cloisonnement du stockage des applications Android, et l'application désactive la sauvegarde cloud d'Android pour ses données. Nous vous recommandons de protéger votre appareil par un verrouillage d'écran.

## The problem

I needed my own place to showcase projects, document my apps' progress and collect feedback, **with zero hosting costs and no servers**.

## The solution

- **Astro** generates a fast static site with great SEO.
- **GitHub Pages + GitHub Actions**: every change on `main` is published automatically.
- **Pages CMS** to edit projects and posts without touching code.
- **Giscus** (GitHub Discussions) for comments and roadmap votes.

**Entwine** is an app for adult couples, played on **two phones**.

- 🎭 **Two roles:** the **Initiator** picks the day's challenge and its mood (romantic, playful, spicy…), and the **Accomplice** uncovers it little by little.
- 🕰️ **Four phases a day** (morning, midday, evening and night), with clues and small check-ins.
- 🔗 **Pairing with a 6-character code**, with no account or password. Only 2 phones fit in a room.
- 🔔 **Discreet reminders:** notifications can show neutral text.
- 🗝️ **Vault:** the couple's pacts, confessions and letters, unlocked with fingerprint or face.

**Privacy:** no ads or analytics. What you share with your partner is synced through Firebase. The details are in the privacy policy.

**Languages:** English, Spanish and French.

Built with Expo / React Native. For adults 18+ only.

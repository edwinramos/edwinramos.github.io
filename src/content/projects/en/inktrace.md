**InkTrace** is a reading journal, not a reader.

- 📖 **Log your progress** by page or chapter with one tap, and time your sessions.
- ✍️ **Your journal:** save quotes, notes and panels.
- 🔥 **Your stats:** streaks, heatmap and a Wrapped-style recap.
- 🔎 **Add titles** by scanning the ISBN or searching by name.
- ☁️ **Works offline and without an account;** optionally sign in with Google to keep a backup in your own Google Drive.

**Privacy:** no ads or analytics: your library and journal stay on your phone, and the optional backup goes to your own Google Drive.

Built with Expo / React Native.

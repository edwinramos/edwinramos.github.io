PlantCare Journal Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

PlantCare Journal ("the app") is a plant care app developed by Edwin Ramos Madera, an independent developer based in the Dominican Republic ("I", "the developer"). The app is free to download and offers an optional paid Premium subscription. You can optionally connect a Google account to back up your garden to your own Google Drive. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and what control you have over it.

1. Summary

  • PlantCare Journal does not require an account or sign-in. Connecting a Google account is optional and is only needed if you want to use the Drive backup.
  • The developer does not operate any servers and does not receive or store your plants, photos, notes or other content.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no Firebase.
  • Your plants, journal and photos are stored on your device. If you connect a Google account, you can also keep a private copy in your own Google Drive (see section 6).
  • Only when you use an AI feature (diagnosis, assistant or evolution report) is the necessary information sent directly to the Google Gemini API, using an API key that you provide yourself.
  • If you subscribe to Premium, the purchase is processed by Google Play. The app only checks your subscription status on your device through Google Play Billing; no other service is involved (see section 5).

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores locally, in its private storage, the information you enter:

  • Registered plants (name, species, soil, lighting, location, health status, watering frequency) and their photos.
  • Care tasks (watering, pruning, fertilizing, etc.), reminder settings and their history.
  • Plant journal entries, notes, tags, humidity estimates, and photos.
  • Saved AI diagnoses and evolution reports.
  • App preferences (such as language) and a cached copy of your Premium status.
  • If you connect a Google account, the name and email of that account, so the app can show which account is connected.
  • Your Google Gemini API key, if you set one. It is kept in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore-backed, through expo-secure-store), separate from the rest of the app data.

This information never leaves your device except as described in sections 4 and 6. The developer has no access to it.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos of your plants when you choose to (when adding a plant or a journal entry, or for a diagnosis). Photos are stored locally.
  • Photo selection: you can pick images from your gallery through the system photo picker; the app only accesses the images you select.
  • Notifications: to remind you about care tasks such as watering. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; the app uses no push server and requests no push token. You can turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Internet: to send AI requests to Google Gemini and, if you connect a Google account, to back up or restore your garden through Google Drive. Purchases go through the Google Play app on your device.
  • Google account (optional): if you choose to connect your Google account for backup, the app requests only the Drive app-data scope and basic profile (name and email). It does not request access to the rest of your Drive.
  • Google Play billing: added by the Google Play Billing library so you can buy the Premium subscription and the app can check whether it is active.
  • Clipboard: only when you tap paste or copy: the app reads the clipboard to paste your Gemini API key, and can copy an AI answer to it. Clipboard content is not stored or sent anywhere else.

The app does not request access to your location, microphone, contacts, or other personal data.

4. AI features (Google Gemini)

The AI features are optional and require you to enter your own Google Gemini API key, obtained from Google AI Studio. Requests go directly from your device to Google (generativelanguage.googleapis.com, over encrypted HTTPS):

  • Diagnosis and assistant: when you request a diagnosis or ask a question, the app sends the plant photo you choose (if any), plant data (name, species, location, watering frequency, relevant history) and the text you write (symptoms, observations or questions).
  • Evolution report (Premium): when you create an evolution report for a plant, the app sends up to 6 photos of that plant, together with its saved diagnoses, journal entries and completed care tasks as text.
  • In every case, your API key is sent to authenticate the request.

This data does not pass through any server operated by the developer. Google's handling of it is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy, under the agreement between you and Google associated with your key. Note that under those terms, for unpaid tiers Google may use submitted content to improve its products. Do not send photos or text containing personal information you do not want to share with Google.

The evolution report is saved on your device. If you export it as a PDF, the PDF is created on your device and you choose where to share it through the Android share sheet; the developer does not receive it.

You can delete your saved key at any time from the Settings screen.

5. Premium subscription (Google Play)

Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google: the app and the developer never see your card or payment details. Google's handling of your purchase is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

To unlock Premium, the app asks Google Play, on your device, whether this Google account has an active subscription, and keeps a cached copy of that status on the device. The app does not send your purchase information to the developer or to any other service, and it uses no third-party subscription service.

As the seller, the developer receives from Google, in Google Play Console, the order records that Google provides for every sale (for example order ID, product, price, date and country). They are used only for sales accounting, refunds and support, never for advertising or tracking.

Premium also includes an optional daily automatic backup to Google Drive, described in section 6. Manual backup and restore remain free.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or from the Premium screen in the app. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

6. Optional backup to Google Drive

Connecting a Google account is optional. The journal works without signing in, and your plants, journal and photos remain on your device.

If you choose to connect your Google account, PlantCare can send a copy of your garden to your own Google Drive, using the Google Drive API (v3), over encrypted HTTPS:

  • The copy includes your plants, care tasks, journal, diagnoses, evolution reports, photos, and reminder, language and seasonal-watering settings.
  • It is stored in Drive's private app data folder for this app. That folder does not appear in the normal Drive interface and is not visible to other apps.
  • The app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope, plus basic profile information (openid, email, profile) so it can show the name and email of the connected account. It does not request the drive, drive.readonly or drive.file scopes, and it does not receive your Google password. The developer does not operate a backup server and does not receive your copies.

Manual backup and restore are free and run only when you tap them.

Automatic daily backup is a Premium feature. It runs at most once per local day, only if you have turned it on, and only while the app is open or returns to the foreground. It does not run in the background.

The app keeps the last 3 complete copies in that folder.

  • Restore replaces the garden on this device (it does not merge it with what is already there), after you confirm. A free user can restore more than 5 plants; the free limit applies only when adding another plant afterwards.
  • After a restore, care reminders are scheduled again on the device (reminder identifiers are device-specific and are not included in the copy).
  • Disconnect signs you out on the phone and leaves both the copies in Drive and the local garden in place.
  • Delete my Drive copies is a separate action that asks for confirmation. It deletes only PlantCare's files in the app data folder, not the garden on your phone and not your other Drive files.

The Gemini API key, OAuth tokens, and purchase or Premium data are not sent to Drive.

The app still disables Android's cloud backup of its data. A copy in Drive is what can survive an uninstall or a new phone, after you connect the same Google account and restore it. Uninstalling or resetting the app deletes the local garden but does not delete your Drive copies.

The only other file the app creates for you is the evolution report PDF, which you choose to share (section 4).

Google's handling of data stored in Drive is governed by the Google Privacy Policy, under the agreement between you and Google for your Google account.

7. Data sharing

The developer does not sell or rent your data and does not share it for advertising. Data reaches third parties only as described above: requests you start to Google Gemini (section 4), purchase processing by Google Play (section 5), and, if you connect a Google account, the copies you keep in your own Google Drive (section 6). Those Drive copies are stored in your private app-data folder; the developer does not receive them.

8. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. If you use the Drive backup, copies also stay in your Google Drive until you delete them. You can:

  • Reset the app to a clean install from Settings. This deletes your plants, journal, photos, diagnoses, reports and API key, and cancels scheduled reminders. It does not delete copies in Drive.
  • Delete your API key from Settings.
  • Clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app, which deletes all of its local data but does not delete copies in Drive.
  • Disconnect your Google account from Settings. This signs you out on the phone and leaves both the local garden and the Drive copies in place.
  • Use Delete my Drive copies from Settings, after confirmation. This deletes only PlantCare's files in the app data folder, not the garden on your phone and not your other Drive files.

Purchase data: the developer keeps no purchase data of its own. Google keeps purchase and subscription records under the Google Privacy Policy. The order records that Google shows the developer in Play Console are kept by Google and cannot be deleted by the developer. For any privacy question or request, email edwinramos.93@gmail.com (if it is about a purchase, include the Google Play order ID, which starts with GPA.). I will reply within 30 days at the latest.

For data already sent to Google Gemini, see Google's policies for your API key.

For copies already stored in Google Drive, use the in-app deletion action described above, or see the Google Privacy Policy for your Google account. The developer cannot delete those copies, because the developer does not have access to them.

9. Security

Communication with Google Gemini, Google Drive and Google Play uses HTTPS. Your API key is kept in the device's secure storage. Local data is protected by Android's app storage sandbox, and the app disables Android cloud backup of its data. We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock.

10. Children

PlantCare Journal is intended for adults 18 and over, in line with the Google Gemini API terms. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

12. Contact

Email: edwinramos.93@gmail.com

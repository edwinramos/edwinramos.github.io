**Ticky** is a personal budgeting app that turns receipts into expenses.

- 📷 **Snap the receipt:** the app reads the merchant, date and total on your phone, then suggests a category that learns from your corrections.
- 📊 **50/30/20 plan:** manage your money with Needs, Wants and Savings, colorful rings and progress bars.
- 🔔 **Alerts and summary:** get notified when you get close to a limit, plus a plain-language weekly summary.
- 🔒 **Your data stays on your phone.** Optionally, you can turn on AI-enhanced scanning with your own Gemini key.

**Privacy:** no accounts, no ads, no analytics: your expenses and photos stay on your phone.

Built with Expo / React Native.

FocusTiny is a clean and lightweight Pomodoro timer built to help you stay focused without distractions.

Whether you are studying, working, coding, reading, or practicing a skill, FocusTiny helps you manage your time using simple work and break sessions inspired by the Pomodoro Technique.

Designed with a minimal dark interface and simple controls, FocusTiny keeps your attention on what matters most: getting things done.

FEATURES

• Pomodoro work & break timer

• Automatic work/break cycles

• Simple and distraction-free design

• Lightweight and fast performance

• Session cycle counter

• Easy customization for work and break duration

• Minimal dark mode UI

• Perfect for study, work, and deep focus sessions

WHO IS IT FOR?

• Students

• Developers

• Artists & creators

• Remote workers

• Writers

• Anyone who wants better concentration and productivity

FocusTiny helps you build better focus habits one session at a time.
